    What you are looking at

    • scan is a choice: it names its branch, and the branch not taken is marked omitted.
    • quarantine is a wait: the brick sits on the shelf until the bomb squad's signal arrives, or fails after its 90 s timeout.
    • defuse has a Retry(limit=3, exponential): a failure goes round the return belt and comes back later — 8 s, 16 s, 32 s.
    • reject accepts only failed parents (on=…, need=1): past the last retry, or when the squad never came, the brick goes there.
    • pack joins both routes: omitted satisfies it, so a sorted brick and a defused one both get packed.
